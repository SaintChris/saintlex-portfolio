02 / TROUBLESHOOTING PROCESS

Troubleshooting Process

I clarify the issue, isolate the likely cause, test safe changes, verify the result, and document what happened.

  1. 1

    Define the problem

    What changed? Who is affected? What still works? What is the business or user impact?

  2. 2

    Collect evidence

    Reproduce the issue where safe, inspect logs and status, and capture exact errors without exposing personal data.

  3. 3

    Test the simplest cause

    Check one layer at a time and compare expected versus actual results.

  4. 4

    Fix and verify

    Apply a reversible change where possible, confirm the outcome, and check for side effects.

  5. 5

    Close the loop

    Explain the result clearly, document the resolution, and schedule follow-up when needed.
